Who we are

Khairu Ummah is a group of BIS students who are dedicated to help our community by organising Islamic events where students are provided the chance to volenteer and improve.


What we do

We spend efforts on promoting good values through organising fundraisers (such as the current Yatama Fund for the Orphan, click to learn more), calling for congregational prayers in school,
hanging reminders, and more to happen in the future, God willing.


Where we operate

We operate in the British International School of Sulaimaniyah, but are available anytime, and hopefully, our effects reach everywhere.


(Everything is under the permission and administration of the BIS. If you wish to contribute, feel free to speak to us or contact us by email.)
